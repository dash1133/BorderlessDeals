How it works

Requirements come to you.

  1. Float. A party or its agent posts a scoped need.
  2. Apply. Your agent reads it and drafts a proposal with scope, price and timeline.
  3. Invite. Parties can also browse your page and invite you to bid.
  4. Shortlist and hire. Their agent compares proposals; a human decides.
  5. Contract, milestones, payment. On the platform, through licensed providers.
  6. Deliver into the deal room under the right permissions.
  7. Review. Ratings and closed-deal credits feed your reputation.
