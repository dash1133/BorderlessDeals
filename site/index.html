The problem

Most companies only ever meet domestic buyers.

Cross-border deals are hard to find and hard to finish. Big companies have banks with global desks. Mid-market owners and their local brokers do not. What holds these deals back is missing information and complexity, not a lack of money.

How it works

One platform, from anonymous teaser to closing.

1 · Be on the map

Your company sits in one directory with buyers, brokers, PE funds and service providers. Think LinkedIn for companies.

2 · Be found

Buyers' AI agents match against your anonymous teaser, and your agent builds a list of buyers who fit.

3 · Close

Hire legal, tax, valuation, financing and accounting providers, and run LOI, due diligence, SPA and closing in one deal room. Think Upwork for deal services.

AI-enabled

Your broker, plus an AI agent that answers buyers abroad in their own language.

Your broker works with an AI agent. It drafts your company page and anonymous teaser from your documents, answers buyers' agents only from information you have approved, in the buyer's language, and handles the follow-ups that slow deals down.

  • Answers only from approved information, with the source cited.
  • In the buyer's language.
  • Anything binding goes to a person. Price, terms and commitments are never decided by the agent.
Reach

Buyers abroad find your teaser where they already search.

At launch, your anonymous teaser can be found on Borderless Deals, in Claude and ChatGPT, and on Google. Buyers browse free and join to sign an NDA or contact your broker. Their AI agent checks fit with yours first.

  • No one cold-emails buyers on your behalf. Every contact is one the buyer chose to make.
Confidential by design

Sell to the world without telling the world.

Information opens in three tiers: the anonymous teaser, then the named company page under NDA, then the data room. You or your broker approve each release, and every disclosure is logged.

  • Buyers pass identity (KYC) and anti-money-laundering checks before names are revealed.
Deals that hold

Find what would break a cross-border deal before the buyer does.

Many broken deals fail on things that were knowable earlier. At every stage from the mandate to the SPA, an AI agent tests the seller's and the buyer's stories for contradictions and gaps, and re-checks the corridor's rules, such as foreign-investment screening and sanctions. A specialist is called in for anything deeper, and the broker decides.

In Axial's review of 65 executed LOIs that broke in 2024, non-QoE diligence findings (21.5%), QoE EBITDA discrepancies (15.4%) and business underperformance (12.3%) caused about half the failures. Deals sat in exclusivity for an average of 58 to 203 days, depending on industry, before they broke.

Straight answers

What owners and brokers ask first.

Can I list my company myself?

Not yet. At launch, companies come through a broker on the platform. Direct listing for owners is planned, after a full background verification.

Can I still sell to US buyers?

Yes. Each mandate can be shown to buyers abroad, to US buyers only, or both. Brokers use one system for both.

Will foreign-investment rules block my deal?

The platform flags likely screening issues at the matching stage, before anyone spends money, and connects you with local legal providers.

Who holds the money?

Licensed escrow and payment providers on the platform. Borderless Deals never holds your money.

Who is behind Borderless Deals?

Borderless Deals is a new company built on deal-execution technology from AgenticPM, an AI agent platform for private equity and transaction advisers.
