Straight answers

What strategic buyers ask first.

Why use this if I already have bankers?

Most deal networks are local. Your bankers rarely see the companies held by small brokerages in other countries. This platform surfaces them and handles much of the cross-border complexity for you.

How do I find targets?

Post your acquisition requirements in plain language. Your AI agent searches every anonymous teaser continuously and brings back ranked matches with reasons and regulatory flags.

How do I know a teaser is real?

Every anonymous teaser comes from a screened broker who confirms the mandate. Brokers are rated publicly on their track record.

What do I see before I join?

Anonymous teasers, including through Claude and ChatGPT. To sign an NDA, contact a broker or enter a process, you join the platform.

Will foreign-investment rules block my deal?

The platform flags likely screening issues at the matching stage, before you spend money, and connects you with local legal providers.

Who handles escrow and cross-border payments?

Licensed escrow and payment providers on the platform. Borderless Deals never holds your money; its agents coordinate the providers.
