Straight answers

What private equity asks first.

What does it cost to look?

Nothing to browse anonymous teasers. You join when you want to engage on a deal.

Can it source add-ons for my portfolio companies abroad?

Yes. Each fund or portfolio company can run a standing thesis against every anonymous teaser on the platform.

Doesn't a broker-led platform push up prices for PE?

At entry, sometimes. But the same global buyers raise your exit prices, add-ons become easier to find, and a strong closing record lets you win on certainty, not just price.

Can I use the platform to exit a portfolio company?

Yes. Through your sell-side broker, list it to qualified foreign strategics and funds who would rarely see it otherwise.
