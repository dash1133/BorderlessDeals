Sell-side brokers

Put forward only deals that will hold, and reach global buyers without cold outreach.

  • Gate the deal from the mandate onward. Your agent tests the seller's story for contradictions, finds missing information and recommends take on, fix or decline.
  • Clear regulatory hurdles early. Basic checks against each country's rules at every gate; an adviser is called in for anything deeper.
  • Handle large volumes of documents consistently. The agent reads, cites and summarizes so your staff can approve quickly.
  • Stay compliant as rules change. Mid-deal regulation changes are detected and you're alerted with the impact.
  • Reach global buyers without cold outreach. Teasers are findable on the platform, in Claude, ChatGPT and Google.
  • Keep every deal on cadence. The agent sets the timetable, orchestrates every party and flags slippage early.
Buy-side brokers

Run every client's thesis against every listing, at once.

  • Pre-qualify sellers and validate your client's thesis through agent-to-agent conversations.
  • Get from interest to an initial take fast. The agent prepares the NDA, reads the deal room and delivers a first assessment.
  • Cross-check against outside evidence. Discrepancies become risk flags before any diligence spend.
  • Explain the US market in your client's language: process, timing, providers, regulatory path.
  • Work at the portfolio level. What the agent learns on one deal compounds across the others. The tenth Japanese buyer is far cheaper to serve than the first.
