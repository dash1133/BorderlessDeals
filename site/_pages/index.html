Why now

A wave of US sellers. A wave of foreign buyers. No bridge between them.

How a match happens

Two agents qualify the fit before either human is disturbed.

No cold outreach. The buyer's agent and the seller's agent check thesis fit, size, proof of funds and regulatory risk. Names are revealed only when both sides opt in.

  • Pull, not push: the receiving agent screens every inquiry against its broker's filters.
  • Hundreds in parallel: qualification runs at a scale no team can match.
  • Logged: principals can see what their agent said, and to whom.

Straight answers

The questions brokers ask first.

Will this platform cut me out?

No. The platform is broker-centric. At launch, sellers come in only through a broker, and Borderless Deals is not a broker itself. It exists to make you more efficient and give you global reach.

Will I lose my client or my fee?

No. You keep your mandate and your client relationship. You decide what is shown, to whom and when. The platform never takes a percentage of your deal.

Is the platform a broker?

No. It is a technology and marketplace platform. Brokers on it hold their own licences.

Who handles escrow and payments?

Licensed escrow and payment providers on the platform. Borderless Deals never holds your money; its agents coordinate the providers.

Will foreign-investment screening kill my deal late?

The platform flags likely screening issues at the matching stage, before anyone spends money, and connects you with local legal advisers.

Can I use it for domestic deals too?

Yes. Each mandate can be visible worldwide, in chosen countries, or to domestic buyers only. One system for both.
