Straight answers

What sell-side brokers ask first.

Will this platform cut me out?

No. The platform is broker-centric. At launch, sellers can only come in through a broker, and the platform is not a broker itself. It exists to make you more efficient and give you global reach.

Will I lose my client or my fee if I post a mandate?

You keep your mandate and your client relationship. You decide what is shown, to whom and when. The platform never takes a percentage of your deal.

What do I get for joining?

One system to register mandates, build anonymous teasers and company pages, find buyers and run each process to closing, for both domestic and cross-border deals.

What does it cost?

Founding brokers get a free seat. After that, brokers pay a subscription and listing fees. The platform never takes a percentage of your deal.

How do buyers judge whether to work with me?

Every broker carries ratings and reviews from clients and counterparties, as on Upwork, plus a record of closed deals.

Can I use it for domestic deals too?

Yes. Each mandate can be visible worldwide, in selected countries, or to domestic buyers only.

How does the platform stop deals collapsing at the last minute?

AI agents act as gatekeepers at every stage, starting with the mandate. They test the seller's and buyer's stories for contradictions and missing information, so the issues that usually surface in diligence come out first.
