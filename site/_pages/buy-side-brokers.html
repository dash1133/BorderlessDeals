Straight answers

What buy-side brokers ask first.

What does it cost to look?

Nothing to browse anonymous teasers. You join when a client wants to sign an NDA, contact a broker or enter a process.

What can I list?

A set of requirements for a client, or a named list of targets you're pursuing on a client's behalf.

Will my client be revealed?

Not until both sides opt in. The two agents pre-qualify each other first, and names are revealed only with consent.

How do I know a teaser is real?

Every anonymous teaser comes from a screened broker who confirms the mandate. Brokers are rated publicly on their track record.

Can wealthy individuals or LPs list themselves as buyers?

No. At launch they take part through a buy-side broker or a fund, which is why your role matters.

Will foreign-investment rules block my client's deal?

The platform flags likely screening issues at the matching stage, before anyone spends money, and connects you with local legal providers.

Who handles escrow and cross-border payments?

Licensed escrow and payment providers on the platform. Borderless Deals never holds your client's money.
