Sell-side brokers Page for sell-side brokers →

Will this platform cut me out?

No. The platform is broker-centric. In the first phase, sellers can only come in through a broker, and the platform is not a broker itself. It exists to make you more efficient and give you global reach.

What do I get for joining?

One system to register mandates, build teasers and company profiles, find buyers and run each process to close, for both local and cross-border deals. Your anonymous teasers also reach anyone using Claude or ChatGPT.

Will I lose my client or my fee if I post a mandate?

You keep your mandate and your client relationship. You decide what is shown, to whom and when. The platform never takes a percentage of your deal.

How do buyers judge whether to work with me?

Every broker carries ratings and reviews from clients and counterparties, as on Upwork, plus a record of closed deals.

Can I use it for domestic deals too?

Yes. Each mandate can be visible worldwide, in chosen countries, or to domestic buyers only.

How does the platform stop deals collapsing at the last minute?

Agents act as gatekeepers at every stage, starting with the mandate itself. They test the seller's and buyer's stories for contradictions and missing information, so the issues that usually surface in diligence come out first.

Buy-side brokers Page for buy-side brokers →

What can I list?

A set of requirements for a client, or a named list of targets you're pursuing on a client's behalf.

Will my client be revealed?

Not until both sides opt in. The two agents pre-qualify each other first, and names are revealed only with consent.

Can wealthy individuals or LPs list themselves as buyers?

No. At launch they take part through a buy-side broker or a fund.

Strategic buyers Page for strategic buyers →

Why use this if I already have bankers?

Most deal networks are local. Your bankers rarely see the companies held by small brokerages in other countries. This platform surfaces them, and handles much of the cross-border complexity for you.

How do I find targets?

Post your acquisition requirements in plain language. Your agent searches every anonymous teaser continuously and brings back ranked matches with reasons and regulatory flags.

How do I know a teaser is real?

Every anonymous teaser comes from a screened broker who confirms the mandate. Brokers are rated publicly on their track record.

What do I see before I join?

Anonymous teasers, including through Claude and ChatGPT. To sign an NDA, contact a broker or enter a process, you join the platform.

Will foreign-investment rules block my deal?

The platform flags likely screening issues at the matching stage, before you spend money, and connects you with local legal advisers.

Who handles escrow and cross-border payments?

Licensed escrow and payment providers on the platform. Borderless Deals never holds your money; its agents coordinate the providers so you don't have to.

Sellers and business owners

Can I list my company myself?

Not yet. At launch, companies are listed through a broker on the platform. Direct listing for owners is planned, after a full background verification.

Will my competitors or employees find out I'm selling?

Teasers are anonymous until you or your broker approve a buyer under an NDA. Every release of information is logged.

Why would a foreign buyer pay more?

Foreign buyers can value market entry, technology or capacity differently, and more bidders means more competition for your company.

Who speaks for my company?

Your broker, with an AI agent that answers routine questions only from information you have approved. Price, terms and anything binding are always decided by people.

Private equity Page for private equity →

What does it cost to look?

Nothing to browse anonymous teasers. You join when you want to engage on a deal.

Can it source add-ons for my portfolio companies abroad?

Yes. Each fund or portfolio company can run a standing thesis against every anonymous teaser on the platform.

Doesn't a broker-led marketplace push up prices for PE?

At entry, sometimes. But the same global buyers raise your exit prices, add-ons become easier to find, and a strong closing record lets you win on certainty, not just price.

Can I use the platform to exit a portfolio company?

Yes. Through your sell-side broker, list it to qualified foreign strategics and funds who would rarely see it otherwise.

Service providers Page for providers →

How do I get work?

Register your specialty and jurisdictions. Deal parties post requirements and you bid on them, as on Upwork, or they invite you directly.

Which services are needed?

Financing, legal, accounting and bookkeeping, tax and valuation first, with diligence, insurance, translation and integration to follow.

Everyone

Which countries are covered?

Sellers at launch are US companies, listed through their brokers. Buyers can be anywhere in the world, and each mandate can be shown worldwide, in selected countries, or to domestic buyers only.

Is my data safe?

Information is released in tiers, with permissions, watermarks and access logs. Agents see only what the party they represent allows.

Is the platform a broker?

No. It is a technology and marketplace platform. Brokers on it hold their own licences.

Who is behind Borderless Deals?

Borderless Deals is a new company built on deal-execution technology from AgenticPM, an AI agent platform for private equity and transaction advisers.
