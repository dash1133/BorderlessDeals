Objections, answered

The questions brokers ask first.

Broker
Will this cut me out?
?
✓No
The platform is broker-centric. In Horizon 1 sellers come in only through a broker, and the platform is not a broker.
Broker
Will I lose my client or my fee?
?
✓Never
You keep your mandate, your client and your fee. The platform never takes a percentage of your deal.
Everyone
Is the platform a broker?
?
✓No
It is a technology and marketplace platform. Brokers on it hold their own licences.
Buyer
Who holds the money?
?
✓Licensed providers
Licensed escrow and payment providers on the platform. Borderless Deals never holds your money.
Broker
Will screening kill my deal late?
?
✓Flagged early
Likely foreign-investment screening is flagged at match time, before anyone spends money.
Broker
Can I use it for domestic deals too?
?
✓Yes
Each mandate can be visible worldwide, in chosen countries, or to domestic buyers only.
